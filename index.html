How To Play

Alright, here's how we're going to play.

We're using a standard 52-card deck. You and your opponent each start with five cards.

The rest of the deck becomes the stock. One card is turned face up to start the discard pile.

On your turn, look at the card on top of the discard pile. You can play a card if it matches either its suit or its denomination.

For example, if the top card is the Queen of Clubs, you can play another Queen or any Club.

Eights are special. You can play an eight at any time, and when you do, you choose the suit that the next player has to follow.

If you can't play, draw cards from the stock until you can make a play. If the stock runs out and you still can't play, you pass.

You are also allowed to draw even when you already have a card you could play.

The first player to get rid of every card in their hand wins the game.

When you win, the cards left in your opponent's hand are worth points: eights are worth 50, face cards and tens are worth 10, aces are worth 1, and the other cards are worth their face value.

That's it. Match the suit or rank, use your eights wisely, and try to get rid of your cards before your opponent does.
