How To Play

Crazy Eights

The goal is to be the first player to get rid of all your cards.

Cards are played by matching the suit or rank of the card on top of the discard pile.

An Eight is wild. When you play an Eight, choose the suit that the next player must follow.

If you cannot play a card, draw from the stock until you can play, or until the stock is exhausted.

You may also choose to draw even when you have a playable card.

When a player gets rid of all their cards, the round ends and points are scored based on the cards remaining in the opponent's hand.
